# STAR story template

Use one file per story. Keep each to about two minutes when spoken.

- **Situation:** context in two sentences.
- **Task:** what you were responsible for.
- **Action:** what you did, in first person, with the key decisions.
- **Result:** measurable outcome, and what you would do differently.

Avoid naming employers, internal systems, customers or confidential numbers
in anything you publish here.
